          Seasonality: repeating price cycles

          Every item is tested for three cycles: daily (24 h, player activity), SkyBlock year (124 h, in-game calendar events) and weekly (168 h). Seasonal indices use the course's seasonal-index procedure (price ÷ centred moving average, averaged per position in the cycle). A cycle only counts as confirmed if it repeats consistently (even vs odd cycles correlate ≥ 0.75), predicts the newest cycle out-of-sample (≥ 20% lower error) and swings at least 2%. The list is sorted so the peaks that come soonest are at the top.
