# Not Enough Flips

**Know what an item is really worth before you buy, and catch the deals other players miss.**

Not Enough Flips is a Hypixel SkyBlock trading helper. It shows prices, verdicts, flips and alerts. **It never buys, bids or clicks for you**, so every trade stays your own.

## What it does

- 💰 **Honest prices in tooltips:** a verdict (GOOD BUY / FAIR PRICE / OVERPRICED) that compares like with like: same stars, recombs, enchants, gems, attributes and skins. If an item's sales are erratic, it says *no reliable price* instead of guessing.
- 📈 **Flip finder** (press E, then FLIP):
  - auction flips priced at what actually sells;
  - live bazaar flips that buy *and* sell fast, with profit/hour that accounts for competing orders;
  - craft flips;
  - a Snipe tab.
- ⚡ **Snipe alerts:** a banner the moment a BIN is listed far under the lowest BIN. It filters out manipulated markets and usually-cheap items. It can open the auction for you; you press Buy.
- 🧾 **Bidder check:** see how many coins the people you're bidding against hold, so you don't overspend. This needs your own free Hypixel API key: `/nef apikey <key>`.
- 🔎 **Bazaar signals:** misprices measured against correlated products, pair spreads and recipe ratios, with plain-language suggestions.
- 🔮 **Forecasts:** 1 h / 6 h / 24 h bazaar price forecasts. You can turn them off.
- 🖥️ **Trade Desk** (`/nef desk`): a simple ERP for your trading. Home (to-do list), Stock, Buying, Selling, Finance and Market.

## Fair play

Not Enough Flips only reads public data and shows it to you. It sends no clicks, automates no purchases or bids, and doesn't play the game for you. Use mods on Hypixel at your own risk, and read Hypixel's rules on allowed modifications.

## Requirements

- Fabric Loader
- Fabric API
- Minecraft 1.21.11, 26.1.x, 26.2.x or 26.3.x (pick the matching file)
